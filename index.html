Week 7 Recap

The Last Unbeaten Falls as Week 7 Rattles the Top of the League

By the League Newsroom · Week 7 Edition

Every perfect season meets its end eventually, and the league's last one was buried in Week 7, just in time for Halloween. Borovay, the only undefeated team left standing, ran into Justin in the week's tightest matchup and took his first loss. Justin now sits atop the Shane Falco Division, and for the first time this season no one in the league is spotless. Spooky.

The shakeup did not stop there. Robby took over the Bobbie Miles Division lead as Jordan fell to Parker, who is climbing the standings after a 1-3 start. Nick's three-game win streak ended against Matt, Charlie finally posted the week's top score, and Ross crept to within a game of .500. Borovay still holds a three-game cushion in the Paul Crewe Division, but the race above him looks very different than it did a week ago. The standings had a few real scares this week.

Matt Survives One of the Closest Games of the Week to Spoil Nick's Streak

Matt stays alive, and he's just one game back of a playoff spot. Bucky Irving rushed for over 100 yards and scored twice. Puka Nacua and Ja'Marr Chase finally lined up for good games, and both caught touchdowns. That was enough to overcome a rough day for Brock Purdy, who had his worst game with 2 interceptions. Final: Matt.

Nick's three-game win streak is over. For a second straight week, Derrick Henry let him down, rushing for just 45 yards. He has fewer than 10 points total over the past two weeks. With Josh Allen on bye, Nick didn't have enough. Drake Maye had another big day, scoring 3 touchdowns for his fourth straight game with three or more. Drake London finally found the end zone. That was about it. Chuba Hubbard, Tetairoa McMillan, Sam LaPorta and Justin Jefferson all failed to reach double digits.

— Colin Audible
Parker Climbs From a 1-3 Start Into the Thick of the Race as Jordan Slips

After a 1-3 start, Parker has won three straight, and his squad is heating up. Javonte Williams is leading the charge. He ran for a season-high 155 yards and a touchdown, and he has scored in six of seven games this year. Christian McCaffrey has now scored in six straight, and he seems to be the only healthy member of the 49ers offense. Then there's the find of the season, Omar Cooper Jr., who has 218 yards and 2 scores over the last two weeks. Will it last? Who knows, but for now, ride the Jet. And don't overlook the Vikings D/ST, which has hit double digits every week but one.

For Jordan, the loss snaps a modest two-game win streak and drops him out of the top spot in the Bobbie Miles Division. There were bright spots. Malik Willis had to start with Lamar Jackson still sidelined, and he delivered a season-best 3 touchdowns. Trey McBride had a monster day, with 10 catches for 111 yards and a score, and Colston Loveland is finally looking like the player many thought he would be, with back-to-back games of 65-plus yards and a touchdown. But the drop-off from Kenneth Walker was steep. He had his worst game by far, running for 36 yards and failing to reach double digits for the first time all year. A two-catch game from Nico Collins didn't help either.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Charlie's Backfield Delivers a First Top Score While Suekoff Sinks

For the first time this year, Charlie posted the top score of the week, and the backs did it. Tony Pollard came in with just 1 touchdown on the season, and he scored 3 times and finished with 155 total yards. Amon-Ra St. Brown bounced back to his usual output with 9 catches for 153 yards. In his first game back, Josh Jacobs had 91 total yards and a score, a midseason boost Charlie needed. Kyren Williams added 121 yards and a touchdown. All three running backs got it done. Charlie snaps a two-game skid.

Suekoff had the lowest score of the week, and it's the same story as the last few weeks. He has lost four straight. Ollie Gordon is making a name for himself with another 100-plus rushing day, his fourth straight game with a touchdown. Even Rhamondre Stevenson had a breakout game, with 125 yards and a score. It wasn't enough to carry the rest of the roster, especially a receiver room where Garrett Wilson and Xavier Worthy combined for 5 catches and 37 yards.

— Ivana Blitz
Robby Takes Over the Bobbie Miles Lead as Gembo's Skid Reaches Six

Robby moves into first place in the Bobbie Miles Division. He won it all in 2022, struggled in 2023, won it all again in 2024, and struggled in 2025. He's back on the upswing in 2026. Jonathan Taylor led the way, topping 100 total yards for a second straight week and scoring his 8th touchdown of the season. Ashton Jeanty might be putting it together, with a touchdown in two straight weeks and a season-high 8 receptions. Zay Flowers had one of his best games, with 8 catches for 137 yards and a score. Joe Burrow is locked in, with back-to-back games of 4 touchdown passes.

Gembo has already waved the white flag on the season, so naturally Bo Nix had his best game, scoring twice and rushing for a season-high 73 yards. Luther Burden caught for 130 yards and a score, and he may be next on the trade block. After winning the first game of the year, Gembo has lost six straight.

— Colin Audible
The Wideouts Carry Justin Past the League's Last Unbeaten Team

In the closest contest of the week, Justin toppled the league's lone undefeated team and seized the lead in the Shane Falco Division. His running backs remain a sore spot, but his receivers more than covered for them. Jaxon Smith-Njigba is a force of nature, with 9 catches, 150 yards and a touchdown, giving him 100-plus yards in six of seven games and 8 touchdowns on the year. Tee Higgins turned in his finest afternoon, with 6 catches, 148 yards and a score. And Caleb Williams, back under center, is improving by the week, throwing for 308 yards and 2 touchdowns.

For Borovay, the unbeaten dream has ended at last, and he won't get to match the 7-0 start he posted in 2019. The great no-show was Patrick Mahomes, who managed a season-low 165 passing yards and a season-low in fantasy points. The rest of the roster did its part. Jameson Williams is on a tear, with scores in two straight games, and CeeDee Lamb found the end zone twice. D'Andre Swift's workload actually grew with Williams' return, as he piled up 90 total yards and scored for the third straight week. Still, Borovay leads the Paul Crewe Division by three games.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Lowest Scorer in the League, Closest to .500: Ross Keeps Hanging Around

Ross owns the league's lowest scoring offense and sits one game under .500 anyway. Quarterbacks are the reason, and they led the way again. Dak Prescott threw 3 touchdowns, and Jared Goff added 300-plus yards and 2 scores of his own. Saquon Barkley finally gave Ross a healthy game, reaching the end zone for the first time this season and hitting double digits. Braelon Allen is soaking up work too, with 134 total yards and a third straight game over 100. The receiver room is still lacking, but the quarterback-and-running-back combination is enough to keep him in the hunt.

Rebecca has been the most disappointing team of the year, and a third straight loss only confirms it. Injuries, trades and poor performances have effectively ended her season. There are a few bright spots. Malik Nabers has four straight games over 110 receiving yards and looks like a building block for the future. Jaylen Warren topped 100 rushing yards for the third time this season. And while Jeremiyah Love didn't play, he looks like a valuable piece for next year.

— Ivana Blitz

Week 8 Matchups
Every matchup means something this week. Division leads are on the line, playoff spots are tightening, and one game is circled in red.
Writer's Record: 20-22 on the season (2-4 in Week 7).

Mahomes Depot vs Get Griddy With It

Game of the week, and it's a monster mash. Both teams are coming off losses, and they are the No. 2 and No. 3 scoring teams in the league, so expect a shootout. Nick is hoping Lamar Jackson plays, because Derrick Henry's production has dropped while the Ravens quarterback has been out. Borovay's receiver room is excellent, with CeeDee Lamb, Jameson Williams and Parker Washington all playing well. Nick has the edge at quarterback with Josh Allen and Drake Maye. That's the difference. Nick survives the night.

Bye Week Players: Borovay: Cam Skattebo, Tyler Shough.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Get Griddy With It

Jayden's Mambo No. 4s vs The Idea of BO-lieve

A team that has dropped six in a row, creeping through the fog toward a team licking its wounds. Gembo has lost six straight, and Jordan is coming off a defeat of his own, with injuries haunting his roster, especially at quarterback. He is praying Lamar Jackson rises from the dead this week. Even if Jackson stays in the crypt, the matchup leans heavily Jordan's way, with Bijan Robinson and a Kenneth Walker who should bounce back after a rare dud. Gembo's only hope is a miracle on Halloween night: Jahmyr Gibbs dropping 40.

Bye Week Players: Jordan: Nico Collins, Mike Evans, CJ Stroud. Gembo: Kaelon Black.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

DAMNNN Daniel!! vs Barking up the Wrong Tree

Potentially the closest matchup of the week, and a real graveyard smash. Two 3-4 teams in the thick of the playoff hunt, both coming off wins, and one of them claws its way back to .500. Ross will keep leaning on his quarterbacks, Dak Prescott and Jared Goff, plus players rising from the injury list, with Saquon Barkley, Ladd McConkey and A.J. Brown back in the mix. Health could bring his season back from the dead. Matt needs his receivers, Puka Nacua and Ja'Marr Chase, to deliver, along with Bucky Irving, who is on a recent hot streak.

Bye Week Players: Matt: Brock Purdy.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: DAMNNN Daniel!!

Fat JaMarcus Russell vs BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

Justin just knocked off the league's last undefeated team, and now he faces another scare. Charlie won too, and a win here gets him back to .500. Both teams have elite receivers: Jaxon Smith-Njigba for Justin and Amon-Ra St. Brown for Charlie. Whichever one shows up decides this game. St. Brown is questionable with an ankle injury, so watch that. Charlie has the better running backs. Justin has the better quarterbacks and more depth overall. Depth wins. Justin survives the night.

Bye Week Players: Justin: George Kittle, Chris Olave. Charlie: Isaiah Likely, Jordan Watkins.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Fat JaMarcus Russell

The Hurts Locker vs Baker's Bunch

Robby has won two straight, and Rebecca has dropped three in a row, but beware, for something stirs in the shadows. Rebecca is a sleeper agent, a team far better than its record suggests, quietly waiting for its moment to rise. Her task is steep, since she must face Jonathan Taylor, the very back she shipped to Robby in a trade, and that ghost now haunts her. Robby holds the edge up and down the lineup, with quarterbacks Joe Burrow and Trevor Lawrence, receiver Zay Flowers, and Ashton Jeanty alongside Taylor. He should roll.

Bye Week Players: Rebecca: Malik Nabers. Robby: Deebo Samuel, Alvin Kamara.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Baker's Bunch

Breece Yourselves vs Unsolicited Dak Pics

Suekoff has lost four straight and is starting to look like a skeleton in the closet. Parker owns the longest active win streak in the league at three games, and his team is clicking, led by Javonte Williams, Omar Cooper Jr. and George Pickens. The catch is that he needs a second running back with Christian McCaffrey on bye. If an upset is coming this week, here's the trick. Suekoff has no real high-powered stars, but Ollie Gordon, Garrett Wilson and even Rhamondre Stevenson can put up good games. They all have to do it at once, or it's Parker's treat.

Bye Week Players: Parker: Christian McCaffrey, Juwan Johnson.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Unsolicited Dak Pics
