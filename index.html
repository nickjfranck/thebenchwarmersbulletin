Week 2 Recap

Two Weeks In, and the League Is Starting to Sort Itself Out

By the League Newsroom · Week 2 Edition

Two weeks doesn't make a season, but it starts to tell you who's paying attention. Three teams — Nick, Justin, and Borovay — are pulling away undefeated, while Parker, Rebecca, and Suekoff are already staring at 0-2 with nowhere left to hide. The middle of the pack got a jolt, too: Charlie pulled off the upset of the week, knocking off a previously unbeaten Jordan, and Davante Adams needed a 36-point Monday night explosion just to keep Robby off the losing side. Borovay, meanwhile, quietly leads the entire league in scoring — still without Brock Bowers, still finding a way. The opening round of division play wraps up next week, which means everyone gets one more shot to settle a score close to home before the schedule sends them looking further out.

The other story of these first two weeks has been quarterbacks dropping like flies. Sam Darnold and Kyler Murray went down in Week 1. Jayden Daniels, Jaxon Dart, and Caleb Williams joined them in Week 2 — Dart's now looking at a season-ending knee injury. Murray looks on track to play. Darnold's still a question mark. Everyone else is left checking the injury report before setting a lineup, not just the box score.

Here's how all six games shook out, division by division — full breakdowns below.

Shane Falco Division

The Blue Bloods
Get Griddy With It Tops Unsolicited Dak Pics

Family get-togethers just got a little more interesting — Nick took down his brother-in-law Parker, 148.52 to 103.52, and he's not going to let him forget it anytime soon. Josh Allen was the story: five total touchdowns, 40 fantasy points on his own. Drew Lock filled in for the injured Sam Darnold and tossed three more. Henry (68 yards, a score) and Hubbard (64 total yards, a score) did the rest. McMillan's still touchdown-less but has 176 yards through two weeks. Nick's 2-0 for the first time since 2023, when it carried him all the way to the championship game. Final: Nick.

Parker's night was undercut early — DJ Moore left with an injury, the second week in a row he's lost a piece early. Javonte Williams managed just 30 yards, Wentz was a game manager at 143. The bright spots: CMC scored twice for 20 points, Ferguson has two touchdowns on the season, and Christian Watson looks like a real breakout — 10 catches, 188 yards, 3 scores. Not enough. It's Parker's first-ever 0-2 start. Moving on.

— Colin Audible
Fat JaMarcus Russell Sends The Hurts Locker to 0-2

Justin's 2-0 for the first time since 2018 — he's never turned that into a 3-0 start, but that's a question for next week. Caleb Williams left late with an injury worth watching. Stafford bounced back with 4 touchdown passes. Smith-Njigba doesn't care who's throwing it — 9 catches, 155 yards, 3 scores. Kittle caught his first touchdown since tearing his Achilles in January. Henderson looked good in his season debut — 76 yards, a score. Final: Justin.

Rebecca's roster held up its end more than the final score suggests. Taylor did his job — 132 total yards, 2 touchdowns — and Kincaid is turning into a real weapon: 7 catches, 95 yards, a score. Herbert wasn't it: 192 yards, 2 picks, a real case to be benched. Nabers caught one pass, Odunze has five all season. And for the second straight week, Bryce Young went off from the bench — 287 yards, 3 touchdowns. Somebody go check if he's available. It's Rebecca's first 0-2 start in her history running this team.

— Colin Audible

Bobbie Miles Division

Ring Row
Baker's Bunch Gets on Track, Edges DAMNNN Daniel!!

Robby trailed heading into Monday night, then let Davante Adams do the talking — 8 catches, 195 yards, 2 touchdowns, a 36-point eruption that turned a deficit into a win and kept him off 0-2. DeVonta Smith added 10 catches and 117 yards, exactly what everyone expected once A.J. Brown left town. Burrow was fine, not fireworks — 207 yards, 2 touchdowns. Trevor Lawrence crashed back to earth after last week's fireworks: 189 yards, no scores, a pick. Robby survives.

Matt's story is a what-if: no Puka Nacua, and a healthy Nacua probably closes this 10-point gap. Ja'Marr Chase looked like himself again — 7 catches, 75 yards, 2 touchdowns, one of them ridiculous. Purdy was excellent for the second straight week — 287 yards, 2 touchdowns, another on the ground — and Kelce turned back the clock for 101 yards and a score. Jalen Coker put up his second straight 8-catch game from the bench — 16 catches, 204 yards, 2 touchdowns on the year — and still hasn't started. Achane got to 10 points but still hasn't found the end zone.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY Hands Jordan His First Loss

Call it the upset of the week. Amon-Ra St. Brown found the end zone twice for the second straight game — 9 catches, 142 yards. Cam Ward wasn't flashy (183 yards) but didn't turn it over and added 2 rushing touchdowns, arguably his best game yet. Kirk Cousins matched him blow for blow — 253 yards, 3 touchdowns — and Stefon Diggs kept producing from the bench: 5 catches, 47 yards, 2 touchdowns, 3 on the year. The one soft spot: MarShawn Lloyd has just 57 rushing yards through two weeks.

Jordan drops his first game, and the injuries are stacking up — Jayden Daniels was carted off with an elbow injury, leaving Stroud and Willis as the options behind center. Lamar Jackson looked human (235 yards, 1 touchdown, an interception), Bijan couldn't crack double digits, Evans got hurt after three catches, and Collins sat with a hamstring issue. Loveland has one catch all season. The bright spot: Kenneth Walker looks like a workhorse — 30 touches, 178 yards this week alone.

— Frank Lee Hurtz

Paul Crewe Division

Contenders & Ghosts
Mahomes Depot Stays Unbeaten, Turns Back The Idea of BO-lieve

Still no Brock Bowers. Still no problem. Borovay's one of three unbeaten teams left, and now leads the league in scoring. CeeDee Lamb led the way — 32 points, 8 catches, 2 touchdowns — and Mahomes wasn't far behind (382 yards, 3 touchdowns, his best start in years). James Cook even stole a touchdown from Josh Allen. Tyler Shough did it again too — 252 yards, 2 touchdowns, zero turnovers. The one real question: the receiver room behind Lamb. Jameson Williams has 2 catches this week, 6 on the season.

For Gembo, it's the same problem as last week — the quarterbacks aren't holding up. Drake Maye is off to a dreadful start (1 touchdown, 4 interceptions, no double-digit games yet), and Bo Nix was only marginally better. Jadarian Price is banged up now too (shoulder, questionable), leaving a thin RB room behind Gibbs, who was a bell cow — 113 total yards, a touchdown. Olave rode the same Saints offense that just torched everybody through Shough (8 catches, 86 yards, a score), and McBride remains the one piece nobody worries about — 8 catches, 41 yards, a touchdown.

— Ivana Blitz
Barking up the Wrong Tree Gets Back on Track, Deals Breece Yourselves Another Loss

The lowest-scoring game of the week, between two 0-1 teams both trying to avoid 0-2 — and Ross came out on top. His quarterbacks were the story: Goff (327 yards, 4 touchdowns) and Prescott (279 yards, 4 touchdowns) combined for 60% of his points, and nobody else reached double digits. Barkley missed time with a stinger and is questionable this weekend; Brooks is banged up too, and now lands on injured reserve — yet another name on Ross's growing injury list. Wicks was a bright spot — 5 catches, 74 yards, no score yet.

Bad week, getting worse. Suekoff's the lowest-scoring team in the league through two weeks, and it looks like he's lost Jaxon Dart for the year with a knee injury — Jacoby Brissett or a trade is next. The roster itself isn't broken: Hampton (94 yards, a touchdown), Wilson (57 yards, a touchdown), and Jones (105 yards) were all solid, and Warren found the end zone again. There's just no one breaking the game open yet. A step forward from Mayfield (1 touchdown through two games) would help — and take Egbuka with him.

— Ivana Blitz

Week 3 Matchups
This week's slate, covered by our three division beat writers: Colin Audible (Shane Falco), Frank Lee Hurtz (Bobbie Miles), and Ivana Blitz (Paul Crewe). The opening round of division play wraps up this week — everyone gets their last crack at a divisional rival before the schedule opens up.
Writer's Record: 6-6 on the season (2-4 in Week 2).

Barking up the Wrong Tree vs Mahomes Depot

Ross narrowly dodged an 0-2 start, but the numbers still aren't pretty — he's bottom two in the league in scoring. Goff and Dak both had strong Week 2s, so the quarterback room isn't the problem anymore; the skill positions are. Saquon Barkley has 11 total points through two weeks, Chase Brown's been fine but unspectacular, AJ Brown's still on IR, and Jonathan Brooks just joined him there. Ladd McConkey needs to be the answer, and soon. Borovay brings the opposite problem into this one — too many good options. He's 2-0, leads the league in scoring, and Brock Bowers could make his season debut this weekend, which should terrify the rest of the division. Mahomes is off to one of his best starts in years, Tyler Shough keeps proving doubters wrong, and Parker Washington is quietly playing like a top-15 fantasy receiver. A win here puts Borovay at 3-0 — his best start since 2019, when he opened the year on a seven-game heater.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Mahomes Depot

Baker's Bunch vs BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

Both sides arrive off a win, and both are leaning on quarterback play to carry them further. Robby's Joe Burrow has been steady rather than spectacular, the kind of floor you can build a season on, while Trevor Lawrence looked every bit the ascending star in Week 1 before crashing back to earth in Week 2 — which version shows up here is anyone's guess. He's still chasing a breakout from Jeremiah Love and a bounce-back from Jeanty, the bell-cow back in Las Vegas who looked the part in Week 1 but not Week 2 — consistency is the theme for this whole roster, and it hasn't shown up yet. The news is good on the perimeter, at least — Zay Flowers is expected to play. Charlie's counting on lightning striking twice: Cam Ward turned in one of his best games of the year last week, and Kirk Cousins did the same right alongside him, and the question is whether either can build on it. Amon-Ra St. Brown is playing like a cheat code right now, but Charlie needs more than one receiver carrying the offense — MarShawn Lloyd or Tony Pollard chipping in behind Kyren Williams would go a long way. The quarterback room tips this toward Robby.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Baker's Bunch

Get Griddy With It vs Fat JaMarcus Russell

Two unbeaten teams enter, one leaves. Justin's never opened a season 3-0 in his history, and now he's chasing it without Caleb Williams — that's the swing in this one. He needs another big week from Matthew Stafford and JSN, and his backfield committee of Travis Etienne, Kyle Monangai, and TreVeyon Henderson to hold up its end without the injured QB bailing anyone out. Nick, on the other hand, has started 3-0 twice before — 2020 and 2023 — and gets a boost if Sam Darnold's back under center. Josh Allen is the engine regardless, and with Murray back, keep an eye on a tick up for Jefferson. With Jonathan Stewart going down, Chuba Hubbard should see even more work, and the real question is whether Drake London finally breaks out with Penix at QB.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Get Griddy With It

Breece Yourselves vs The Idea of BO-lieve

This one might matter more than a Week 3 game should for Suekoff. Start 0-3, and the conversation shifts from this season to next. It's not getting easier — Jaxon Dart now looks done for the year, and there isn't much hope left on this roster. Omarion Hampton's been steady, Tyler Warren keeps finding the end zone even without piling up yardage, but the real fix has to come from Baker Mayfield, averaging under 12 points a game, which is dragging down Emeka Egbuka right along with him — he hasn't cracked double digits all season. Gembo's issue is simpler and somehow worse: it starts and ends at quarterback. Jahmyr Gibbs, Chris Olave, Trey McBride, even Jaylen Waddle are all doing their jobs — Drake Maye and Bo Nix just aren't doing theirs. And if Jadarian Price can't go, it's an open question who steps in at RB2.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: The Idea of BO-lieve

DAMNNN Daniel!! vs Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

The matchup of the week, and both sides are coming off a loss. Matt played Week 2 without Puka Nacua, but he should be back to pair with Ja'Marr Chase, who put together a big week of his own. Bucky Irving has been finding him through the air, and now Matt just needs De'Von Achane to find paydirt — Travis Kelce, fresh off a 100-yard game and clearly locked in with Patrick Mahomes, gives him another way to win. Jordan's dealing with more uncertainty: with Jayden Daniels out, C.J. Stroud steps in at QB2, and he's off to a solid enough start this year. The bigger question is whether Nico Collins and Mike Evans both suit up — both are questionable heading into the night. Kenneth Walker's giving Jordan everything he could ask for, but he needs a bounce-back from Bijan Robinson, and somebody needs to start throwing Colston Loveland the ball. If Collins and Evans are both out, this one tilts hard toward Matt.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: DAMNNN Daniel!!

Unsolicited Dak Pics vs The Hurts Locker

Both 0-2, and neither has ever started a season this poorly. One of them fixes that Sunday — the other is staring down 0-3. The running backs are a wash: Rebecca rolls out Jonathan Taylor and Breece Hall, Parker counters with Javonte Williams and CMC, and all four should get theirs. So this one comes down to the receivers, and that's where the questions pile up. Can Pickens finally step up for Parker? Does DJ Moore even suit up? On the other side, Malik Nabers is questionable and breaking in a new quarterback, and so is Rome Odunze — Rebecca needs one of them to hit. She's also facing the same question that's dogged her all season: is this the week Justin Herbert finally sits for Bryce Young? At 0-2, something's got to give.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: The Hurts Locker

The Largest Week 2 Margin of Victory in League History

Gembo's roster that week was led by Blake Bortles, DeAngelo Williams, Larry Fitzgerald, and Rob Gronkowski. Somewhere, a decade later, Gembo is hoping his current quarterback situation turns out half as well.
