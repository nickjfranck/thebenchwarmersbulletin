Week 6 Recap

Fresh Faces Deliver as the League Splits Into Buyers and Sellers

By the League Newsroom · Week 6 Edition

Six weeks in, the league has sorted itself into two camps. On one side are the buyers, and the trade market is already paying dividends. Borovay is still the lone unbeaten team at 6-0, with Nick and Justin right behind at 5-1. Their new additions went straight to work. Drake Maye made his Nick debut with three touchdown passes. Bhayshul Tuten scored twice in his first game with Justin, with Chris Olave adding a touchdown and Caleb Williams returning with over 300 yards. Robby's newcomers, Jonathan Taylor and Matthew Golden, helped him run away from Suekoff. It was a good week to be shopping.

On the other side are the sellers, and the standings are not kind. Rebecca, Gembo and Suekoff are all 1-5, and Rebecca will play out the season without Jalen Hurts. In the middle, Monday night did the sorting. Jordan staged a comeback behind two second-half touchdown passes from Jayden Daniels and now sits atop the Bobbie Miles Division at 4-2. Christian McCaffrey sealed Parker's win and pushed him back to .500, while Matt, Charlie and Ross are all stuck at 2-4. The buyers are getting paid, and the sellers are looking ahead to next year.

Drake Maye Makes a Splashy Debut as Nick Dispatches a Shorthanded Ross

Nick has won three in a row. In his first game with the team, Drake Maye showed why Nick moved draft capital for him. He threw 3 more touchdowns and led the team in scoring. That's 10 touchdown passes in the last three weeks, and the early-season struggles are well in the past. Josh Allen was happy he didn't have to carry the load, finishing with 2 scores. Chuba Hubbard rushed for 120-plus yards for the second straight game and added a touchdown. There was one small concern. Derrick Henry ran for a season-low 58 yards and failed to score for the first time. Final: Nick.

Ross was shorthanded, and it showed. Michael Penix had his best game, throwing for 225 yards and 3 touchdowns, and Braelon Allen topped 100 total yards. That's where the good news ends. A.J. Brown looked rusty in his return, with 3 catches for 27 yards. Ladd McConkey played a few snaps before getting hurt again. And for a second straight week, Dak Prescott threw 2 interceptions and failed to top 14 points.

— Colin Audible
Jordan Rallies Late While Rebecca Wonders What Might Have Been

Trailing going into Monday night, Jordan needed a late rally, and Jayden Daniels delivered it. He tossed two second-half touchdowns to complete the comeback and send Jordan to his second straight win. At 4-2, he sits atop the Bobbie Miles Division. His stars did enough. Kenneth Walker rushed for 128 yards and 2 scores, and C.J. Stroud threw for 312 yards and 3 touchdowns. Even a quiet week from Bijan Robinson, who ran for just 61 yards and hasn't scored in two weeks, couldn't slow Jordan down.

For Rebecca, this one will sting. She has lost two in a row and is now 1-5, and the cruelest part is how close it was. Had she kept Jonathan Taylor, whom she traded to Robby, she would have won. Instead she started Tyjae Spears, who rushed for 1 yard, and the player she got back in the deal, Jeremiyah Love, missed the game with an ankle injury. Malik Nabers was brilliant again, notching a third straight 100-yard game and a third straight game with more than 21 points. The bigger blow came off the field. Jalen Hurts is out for the season, so Justin Herbert will start from here on out. For a team that may not be gunning for the title anymore, it's a quiet end to the dream.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Christian McCaffrey Closes It Out as Parker Climbs Back to .500

The numbers say Parker earned this one, and Christian McCaffrey did the closing on Monday night. He ran for 115-plus yards and 2 touchdowns, and he has now scored in five straight games. Jordan Love was inefficient, completing just 56% of his passes, but he still threw for 276 yards and 2 touchdowns for his third 20-plus-point game. With DJ Moore sidelined, Keon Coleman scored for a third straight game, and Javonte Williams found the end zone for the fourth week in a row, giving him 8 touchdowns on the year. Parker is back to 3-3.

Matt is 2-4, and the box score shows two stars cooling off at once. Bucky Irving was held to 46 yards a week after a 31-point explosion. Puka Nacua came back down to earth with 6 catches, a week after he caught 17. Deshaun Watson had his worst game of the season, with 164 yards and no touchdowns. Brock Purdy kept his steady pace with 264 yards and a score, and Jalen Coker returned from a missed game with 6 catches for 97 yards. It wasn't enough.

— Ivana Blitz
Borovay Wins an Off Week and Stays Unbeaten

Not his best week, but it was enough. Tyler Shough bounced back from a negative week with his best game, four touchdowns, three passing and one rushing. James Cook rushed for 95 yards and a score, topping 20 points for the fourth time and scoring for a fifth straight week. D'Andre Swift had his best game since Week 1, finding the end zone twice. One thing to watch: Cam Skattebo didn't touch the ball. Borovay may need an answer there. He's 6-0. Final: Borovay.

Another low-scoring outing for Charlie. Only three players reached double digits. Isaiah Likely led the way again with 6 catches, 99 yards and a touchdown. Kirk Cousins kept up his fantastic season, throwing for over 300 yards and 2 scores, and Stefon Diggs even caught a touchdown. The rest didn't help. Kyren Williams failed to reach double digits for the first time, rushing for just 50 yards. MarShawn Lloyd ran for 10 yards a week after scoring twice. The next question is Josh Jacobs. He's been removed from the Commissioner's Exempt List and can return to practice. Will he play? He could be a midseason game changer.

— Colin Audible
Robby's Newcomers Ball Out While Suekoff's Season Slips Away

Robby didn't just beat Suekoff. He buried him, and he stays right in the thick of the division race. Trevor Lawrence delivered the best game of his season, throwing for over 300 yards and 4 touchdowns. The two newcomers in Robby's lineup this week both balled out. Matthew Golden kept up his torrid pace with 6 catches for 104 yards and a score, and Jonathan Taylor added more than 150 total yards and a touchdown. Those three were nearly enough on their own. The only cloud is that Davante Adams ruptured his hamstring, so Robby will have to find a replacement.

For Suekoff, the slide reached three straight, and the box score reads like a bad dream. His kicker, Tyler Bass, led the team in points, and that is never a good sign. Seven of his starters scored fewer than 7 points. Robby's bench came close to outscoring his entire starting lineup. The lone bright spot was Emeka Egbuka, who scored for a second straight week and looks to be crawling out of his early-season funk. Beyond that, there wasn't much to celebrate.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Tuten and Williams Debut as Justin Routs Gembo

Justin owned the league's top score this week, and his new acquisitions went straight to work. Bhayshul Tuten impressed his new GM with 2 touchdowns and led the team in scoring. Chris Olave caught a score on 4 receptions. Caleb Williams returned and threw for over 300 yards and a touchdown, and there's room for him to grow. Jaxon Smith-Njigba did what he does best, catching 12 balls for 134 yards. The only thing missing was a touchdown. Germie Bernard went off for 11 catches, 87 yards and a score. Justin improves to 5-1, and his only remaining problem is his RB2 spot.

Gembo made multiple moves last week and has signaled he's looking toward next year. The performance showed it. The team scored well under 100. Bo Nix was bad again, throwing for just 107 yards, which dragged down Jaylen Waddle, who had 4 catches for 24 yards. One bright spot was Carnell Tate, who had 6 catches for 70 yards and his first touchdown of the season. Even with Cam Ward at quarterback, he's showing growth. Gembo falls to 1-5.

— Ivana Blitz

Week 7 Matchups
Your beat writers went a perfect 6-0 last week, so the pressure is officially on. The Game of the Week pits the league's last unbeaten team against the team with just one loss, and with byes in play, the depth charts will matter more than ever. Two teams are fighting to climb back into the race, and a few more are fighting to stay out of the cellar.
Writer's Record: 18-18 on the season (6-0 in Week 6).

Get Griddy With It vs DAMNNN Daniel!!

Nick has won three straight and owns the league's best offense. Matt, at 2-4, is fighting to crawl back into the race, and he needs a win. The wrinkle: Nick is without Josh Allen, who is on bye. Drake Maye steps in at QB1, and either Geno Smith or Sam Darnold steps in at QB2. Justin Jefferson caught a touchdown in his last game. Is he finally ready to explode with Kyler Murray? Matt gets Ja'Marr Chase back from his own bye, and he can pair him with Puka Nacua and a steady Brock Purdy. That's a dangerous trio. It's still not enough to match Nick's depth. Nick takes it.

Bye Week Players: Nick: Josh Allen.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Get Griddy With It

Jayden's Mambo No. 4s vs Unsolicited Dak Pics

Two teams, two straight wins, and one prize: a spot in the middle of the playoff conversation. Parker climbed back to .500 on a big Monday night from Christian McCaffrey, who has scored in five straight games. Jordan sits atop the Bobbie Miles Division at 4-2, with Kenneth Walker and Bijan Robinson in the backfield. Both teams will have byes to navigate. Jordan will be without Jayden Daniels, but he might get Lamar Jackson back, and C.J. Stroud is ready to carry the passing game either way. Parker is without DJ Moore and Keon Coleman, leaving Jordan Love and Christian Watson to lean on. It should still be a shootout, and the team with more ways to score gets the nod. That's Jordan.

Bye Week Players: Jordan: Jayden Daniels. Parker: DJ Moore, Keon Coleman.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY vs Breece Yourselves

This could be the lowest-scoring game of the week. Charlie averages just 112.8 a game and Suekoff 105.8, but a win would put Charlie back in the playoff hunt at 3-4. Charlie has lost Terry McLaurin for the season, but he gets Amon-Ra St. Brown back, with Isaiah Likely and Kirk Cousins humming. Josh Jacobs has been removed from the Commissioner's Exempt List and could technically play. We'll see. Suekoff, 1-5 and losers of three straight, might get Baker Mayfield back, but he's also open to trading everyone, so we'll see who's still on the roster by Sunday. This one leans Charlie.

Bye Week Players: Charlie: Stefon Diggs. Suekoff: Omarion Hampton.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

Baker's Bunch vs The Idea of BO-lieve

Opposite ends of the spectrum. Robby is rolling at 4-2, with Jonathan Taylor and Matthew Golden showing up in a big way. Trevor Lawrence is on bye, so Robby may turn to J.J. McCarthy. Davante Adams is on IR, but DeVonta Smith and Zay Flowers can fill in. Gembo has all but given up on 2026, and everyone is available, including Jahmyr Gibbs. The price is steep: your house, your cars and the naming rights to your next kid. On paper, it's a mismatch. Robby.

Bye Week Players: Robby: Trevor Lawrence.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Baker's Bunch

Mahomes Depot vs Fat JaMarcus Russell

The Game of the Week, and something has to give. Borovay is the league's last unbeaten team at 6-0. Justin has lost just once, and he's been scoring at a ferocious rate with his new additions. The battle may come down to running backs, since James Cook is on bye for Borovay and Bhayshul Tuten is on bye for Justin. That leaves Borovay to lean on D'Andre Swift, with Brock Bowers and a questionable CeeDee Lamb leading the passing game. Justin has Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Chris Olave and Caleb Williams. It's hard to pick against a team that hasn't lost yet. Borovay by a nose.

Bye Week Players: Borovay: James Cook. Justin: Bhayshul Tuten.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Mahomes Depot

The Hurts Locker vs Barking up the Wrong Tree

Rebecca's season is all but over at 1-5, while Ross, at 2-4, is technically just one game back of a playoff spot. With Jalen Hurts out for the year and Justin Herbert on bye, Rebecca will be scrambling at quarterback, with Bryce Young in line to step in. What she does have is Malik Nabers, who has three straight 100-yard games. Ross has Emmanuel Wilson scoring in back-to-back games, but Saquon Barkley's health is a constant concern. The edge goes to the team with the hot receiver. Rebecca.

Bye Week Players: Rebecca: Justin Herbert, Dalton Kincaid. Ross: Ladd McConkey.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: The Hurts Locker
