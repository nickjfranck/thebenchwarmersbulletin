Week 8 Recap

Borovay Bounces Back, Charlie Climbs and the Playoff Race Tightens at the Midway Mark

By the League Newsroom · Week 8 Edition

One loss was all it took to teach Borovay a lesson. The league's best team answered its first defeat with a statement win over Nick, behind three more touchdowns from D'Andre Swift, a pair from Brock Bowers and a Patrick Mahomes bounce-back. Justin dropped a close one to Charlie but still sits atop the Shane Falco Division, and Robby kept pace in the Bobbie Miles Division with a blowout of Rebecca powered by a season-best afternoon from Joe Burrow.

Eight weeks in, the middle of the pack is getting crowded. Charlie and Matt both climbed back to .500, Gembo pulled off an upset over a Jordan team that lost Lamar Jackson to injured reserve, and Parker won again to push his streak to four. Nick has lost two straight, and Jordan holds the sixth and final playoff spot with Charlie and Matt right behind him. Six weeks remain, and the standings will not stay quiet.

Borovay Answers His First Loss With a Statement Win Over Nick

One loss, no panic. Borovay is back in the win column. D'Andre Swift scored three more times and now has 10 touchdowns on the season, tops in the NFL. Brock Bowers caught 7 passes for 2 scores. Patrick Mahomes rebounded from his worst week with 321 yards and 3 touchdowns. The Jaguars D/ST joined in: 4 sacks, 2 interceptions, a touchdown and a fumble recovery, and only 7 points allowed. Final: Borovay.

A solid game from Nick was not enough. The quarterbacks did their job, with Josh Allen and Drake Maye each throwing 3 touchdowns. Chuba Hubbard keeps rolling, with 119 yards and a score. The wide receivers let him down. Drake London had 1 catch, Sam LaPorta had 3, and Tetairoa McMillan caught 6 for just 58 yards and no touchdown. That is a second straight loss.

— Colin Audible
A Tanking Gembo Stumbles Into an Upset as Jordan's Quarterback Vanishes

In a season already written off, Gembo found a sliver of sunshine. Call it a squeaker, a low-scoring win that came more by accident than by design. Jahmyr Gibbs, as always, led the way, rushing for 153 yards and a touchdown. Bo Nix turned in one of the best performances of his season, throwing for 222 yards and 2 scores, one of them to his real-life teammate and fantasy teammate, Jaylen Waddle. It was less a triumph for Gembo than a stumble by his opponent, but a win is a win.

Jordan was undone before the game began. Lamar Jackson was hurt and missed the entire contest, and his zero in the quarterback slot was enough to sink the afternoon. Jackson is now on injured reserve and could be done for the season. Bijan Robinson had one of his worst games and has not found the end zone since Week 4, and Kenneth Walker turned in his second-worst outing of the year. The low scores added up to a loss by about a touchdown, a second straight defeat, and a slip to two games behind in the division.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Matt's Skill Players Do the Heavy Lifting in a Battle of Teams Fighting for .500

The numbers say Matt's offense runs on three players, and all three showed up. Puka Nacua caught 11 passes for 80 yards and a touchdown. Ja'Marr Chase matched the volume with a season-high 11 catches for 141 yards and a score, his third straight game in the end zone. Bucky Irving has now topped 100 rushing yards in three of his last four games, including a season-high 170 yards and a touchdown. The result is a return to .500.

Ross got a glimpse of a healthier roster, but the quarterback let the day slip away. Saquon Barkley scored for a second straight week. Ladd McConkey had his first double-digit game since Week 1, with 4 catches for a season-high 86 yards. Tucker Kraft had his best game of the year, with 6 catches for 53 yards and a score. Then Jared Goff posted a season-low 176 yards, including his first interception of the year, and that was the difference.

— Ivana Blitz
Cousins, Ward and Pollard Push Charlie Back to .500

Charlie had the top score of the week for the second straight week, and he is back to .500. Kirk Cousins threw 4 touchdowns, a season high, and added a rushing score. He has been a top-5 fantasy quarterback all year. Cam Ward had a season-best 304 yards and 2 touchdowns. Tony Pollard is on a run, with a second straight 100-yard game, 2 more touchdowns and 5 in the last two weeks. Josh Jacobs returned and scored twice. Final: Charlie.

It was not a bad week for Justin. He was a top-3 scorer and still ran into a buzzsaw. Tee Higgins had 10 catches for 155 yards and 2 touchdowns. Jaxon Smith-Njigba posted a fourth straight 20-plus point game, with 6 catches for 118 yards and a score. Bhayshul Tuten had a season-high 104 rushing yards and 2 touchdowns. Justin still sits atop the division.

— Colin Audible
A Burrow Barrage Buries Rebecca as Another Injury Piles On

It was a blowout, and nobody was surprised. Robby's team rolled over Rebecca even with Zay Flowers and DK Metcalf contributing less than a single point apiece. Joe Burrow did the heavy lifting, throwing for a season-high 462 yards and 5 touchdowns, and adding a rushing score for good measure. Trevor Lawrence tossed 3 more touchdowns. Harold Fannin Jr. exploded for a season-high 10 catches and 154 yards, and Matthew Golden hauled in 79 yards and a score. Those four alone were enough to settle the afternoon.

For Rebecca, the losing streak has reached four. Jaylen Warren continues to do his job, rushing for a season-high 143 yards and a touchdown, and DeMario Douglas has been quietly good, with 4 touchdowns in his last 4 games. After that, the cupboard was bare. Recent acquisition Jeremiyah Love is headed to injured reserve, where he will join Jalen Hurts.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Parker Survives an Ugly One as Suekoff's Skid Hits Five

Parker's win was not pretty, but a win is a win, and he now has the longest active win streak in the league at four games. Jordan Love is finding his groove, throwing for 288 yards and a season-high 3 touchdowns. Kyler Murray, while still not pretty, had his best fantasy game of the year, with 2 touchdowns. The one real cost was Christian Watson, who broke a rib after catching a season-high 8 passes for 113 yards.

Suekoff posted the lowest score in the league for a third straight week, and there is not much to celebrate. The kicker, Tyler Bass, was his highest-scoring player, followed by the Bears D/ST. Only two position players reached double digits. Garrett Wilson had 6 catches for 83 yards, and Rhamondre Stevenson ran for a season-high 83 yards. It was not enough, and Suekoff has now lost five straight.

— Ivana Blitz

Eight weeks are down and the playoff picture is coming into focus. Parker and Robby meet in the matchup of the week, and the trade deadline is Nov. 20. Every game moves the standings, so make your picks carefully.

Get Griddy With It vs The Idea of BO-lieve

Gembo is coming off a surprise win. Nick has lost two straight. Gibbs, the top running back in the league, always gives Gembo a chance at an upset. The rest of the roster is up and down. Nick has the best quarterback combination in the league in Josh Allen and Drake Maye. His receivers need a bounce-back showing. Quarterbacks decide this one.

Bye Week Players: Gembo: Carnell Tate.

Barking up the Wrong Tree vs Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

Two teams, each clinging to something. Jordan has dropped two straight, and a loss would send him below .500 for the first time all year. Ross is still the lowest-scoring team in the league and hanging on by a thread. Quarterback injuries have wounded Jordan, though he would do well not to mention injuries to Ross, whose A.J. Brown might be out again. Jordan has the stronger running backs in Bijan Robinson and Kenneth Walker, and that should be enough.

His team leads the league in scoring, and everything is clicking, from D'Andre Swift and James Cook to Patrick Mahomes and Brock Bowers. Matt has the receivers to keep it interesting. Borovay has everything else.

Bye Week Players: Matt: Michael Pittman. Borovay: Aaron Rodgers.

BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY vs The Hurts Locker

It's hard to say how long Charlie's run lasts, but as long as Kirk Cousins keeps throwing touchdowns and Kyren Williams and Josh Jacobs keep scoring them, it is tough to pick against him. Rebecca is beat up and has put Jeremiyah Love on injured reserve.

Unsolicited Dak Pics vs Baker's Bunch

Matchup of the week. Parker owns the league's best active streak at four wins. Robby has won three straight. Something has to give. Parker's roster is full of questionable tags, including Christian McCaffrey and George Pickens, and the drop-off without them would be steep. Both teams are hot, with Joe Burrow and Trevor Lawrence slinging it for Robby. This should be the closest game of the week, a true toss-up that tips to the more complete roster.

Bye Week Players: Parker: Wan'Dale Robinson. Robby: DK Metcalf, Pat Freiermuth.
