Week 9 Recap

Upsets Rule Week 9 as Matt Topples Borovay and Gembo Stuns Nick

By the League Newsroom · Week 9 Edition

The shootout of the week lived up to the billing. Matt posted the top score in the league and knocked off Borovay, who put up the second-highest score and still lost. Borovay remains in control of the Paul Crewe Division, but the loss tightened the race for the top seed. Justin cruised past Suekoff to stay on top of the Shane Falco Division, with Parker, riding the league's best five-game winning streak, now just a game back.

The biggest surprise came when Gembo took down Nick, who has now lost three straight and failed to top 100 points for the first time this season. Jordan blew out Ross to get back above .500, Rebecca picked up just her second win, and Charlie fell back under .500 with a loss. The playoff picture is far from settled, with a crowded group at 5-4 chasing the final spots and the trade deadline coming on Nov. 20.

Gembo Beats Another Contender and Stays Within Reach of a Playoff Spot

Gembo has now won two straight against contenders. It was not an explosive game from a manager who signaled weeks ago that he is looking at next year, but it was enough. Luther Burden led the way with 7 catches for 97 yards and a touchdown, his third straight game with a score. Bo Nix threw for 237 yards and 2 touchdowns. Gembo is now within spitting distance of a playoff spot. Final: Gembo.

Nick failed to top 100 points for the first time this season. Another bad week from Derrick Henry sunk him. Henry ran for just 57 yards and has failed to top 6 points in three of his last four games. Josh Allen had his worst game of the year against the Minnesota defense, and that was enough to send Nick to a third straight loss. The bright side: Justin Jefferson might get back on track with Kirk Cousins traded to Minnesota.

— Colin Audible
Jordan's Backs Roar Back as Another Quarterback Falls

This is the kind of showing Jordan's roster is capable of on any given Sunday. In a blowout, he snapped a two-game losing streak and climbed back above .500. Kenneth Walker got back on track, with more than 140 total yards and 3 touchdowns. Bijan Robinson was right behind him, with 147 total yards and a score, his first since Week 4. The hiccup was another quarterback injury, as Malik Willis was placed on injured reserve. The good news is that CJ Stroud, who sat on the bench this week, had his best game of the year with 5 touchdowns. Jordan will roll with Stroud and Jayden Daniels at quarterback from here.

Ross had another subpar showing. His team is the lowest scoring in the league, and he failed to top 100 points for the fourth time this year. Jared Goff had a season-worst game and has just 1 touchdown and 2 interceptions over the last two weeks. Saquon Barkley scored for a third straight week, but he rushed for only 22 yards, and he is touchdown-dependent at this point. Ladd McConkey was hurt again, this time in warmups, and never saw the field. Dak Prescott was the lone bright spot, throwing for 335 yards and 2 touchdowns.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Everything Clicks for Matt in a Shootout With the League's Top Scorer

Matt posted the top score of the week, and everything clicked, from the receivers to the defense. Ja'Marr Chase had a season-high 155 yards and a touchdown, his fourth straight game with a score. Puka Nacua matched him with 150 receiving yards and a score, though the Rams have since announced he will be out for some time with a knee injury. Bucky Irving keeps rolling, with more than 100 total yards and 2 touchdowns. The result is Matt's third straight win.

There was not much more Borovay could have done. His team was the second-highest scorer of the week, and it still lost. Sometimes it just happens. CeeDee Lamb had a season-high 195 yards and a touchdown. Brock Bowers topped 100 receiving yards for the third time and added a score. James Cook rushed for more than 100 yards and scored for the eighth straight game. Borovay is still sitting pretty, and he grew his lead as the league's top scorer.

— Ivana Blitz
A Low-Scoring Slog Ends With a Win for Rebecca and a Step Back for Charlie

It has been a tough season for Rebecca, and a win was overdue. It was only her second of the year, in a low-scoring game where neither team broke 100. Bryce Young had his best game in weeks, throwing for 276 yards and 3 touchdowns. DeMario Douglas is carving out a role in New England, topping 100 yards for the first time. Breece Hall returned and totaled 87 yards. It was not pretty, but it was enough. Final: Rebecca.

Charlie was trying to get back above .500 and instead fell back under it, with his two-game win streak snapped. Josh Jacobs hurt his finger early and left without much impact. Kyren Williams rushed for just 45 yards. Kirk Cousins threw a season-high 3 interceptions. The one bright spot was Jonah Coleman, who scored for a second straight week with 88 total yards and is starting to look like a must-start running back.

— Colin Audible
Parker Rolls to Five Straight and Closes In on the Division Lead

Parker keeps the good vibes going, extending the league's best winning streak to five games by taking down Robby. It was a top-heavy performance, and every manager loves to know they have the players who can deliver one. Jordan Love threw for 372 yards and 3 touchdowns, and added a rushing score. When Love does well, so does Christian Watson, who had a season-high 171 receiving yards and a touchdown. The running back tandem of Christian McCaffrey and Javonte Williams was the chef's kiss: McCaffrey had 87 yards and 2 touchdowns, and Williams ran for over 100 yards and scored. Parker is now within a game of the division lead.

The loss snaps Robby's three-game winning streak. He benefited from Love's big day too, but probably wished he was not playing against him. Matthew Golden had a season-best game with 10 catches for 164 yards and a touchdown. Joe Burrow backed him up with 3 touchdown passes and a rushing score, and even the Bills D/ST got to 20 points for a second straight week. The running back room was bad again. Amid all the trade talk, Jonathan Taylor is staying put in Indianapolis, and he rushed for just 46 yards. Ashton Jeanty's roller-coaster season continued, and he rushed for just 25.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Same Old Song for Suekoff as Wentz Has a Day to Forget

It was not the most exciting matchup, but Justin did more than enough to beat one of the league's worst teams. Tee Higgins continues to find the end zone, with 5 touchdowns over the last 3 weeks. Chris Olave is playing well too, with 7 catches for 98 yards this week. Nothing else really stood out. Caleb Williams threw a season-high 4 interceptions, but he saved a decent day with 2 touchdowns. Matthew Stafford has fallen off in recent weeks, though he did throw for 228 yards and a score. He has thrown an interception in three straight games and has 9 on the season.

Same old song and dance for Suekoff, whose losing streak is now six games. KC Concepcion had a nice game, with 85 yards and his first touchdown of the season. Rhamondre Stevenson also scored, then left with an injury. And then there was Carson Wentz. He threw for 81 yards with 2 interceptions and a fumble, a showing so bad that the Vikings went out and traded for Kirk Cousins.

— Ivana Blitz

Week 10 Matchups
Nine weeks are down and just five regular-season games remain, with the trade deadline on Nov. 20. The league's top two scoring teams meet when Borovay faces Jordan, while Nick and Robby square off in a division clash with real playoff stakes. Make your picks carefully, because every game now matters in the race for a spot.
Writer's Record: 25-29 on the season (2-4 in Week 9).

Get Griddy With It vs Baker's Bunch

Two of the top five scoring teams in the league meet with the division race tightening. Robby holds a one-game lead and is coming off a loss, while Nick has dropped three straight and sits two games back. Inconsistency is the problem on both sides. Robby is waiting on Ashton Jeanty and Jonathan Taylor to settle in at running back. Nick needs a boost from Tetairoa McMillan and Justin Jefferson at receiver. The quarterbacks are the story, with Joe Burrow and Trevor Lawrence facing Josh Allen and Drake Maye, four top-10 passers in one matchup. The stakes are real for both. A win gives Robby breathing room in the division, and a fourth straight loss would put Nick in danger of slipping out of the playoff picture. Somebody has to pick up the slack, and Jefferson, with Kirk Cousins throwing to him, gets the breakout game.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Get Griddy With It

Mahomes Depot vs Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

Good meets good when the league's top two scoring teams collide, and there is hardly a gap to be found between them. Borovay owns the edge at receiver, while Jordan counters with the better backfield. The quarterbacks are a wash, and so are the tight ends, with Brock Bowers and Trey McBride cancelling each other out. Even the defenses are a mirror image, two top-five units bumping heads. In a matchup this even, it will come down to the fringe players making it happen, and that leans Borovay.

Bye Week Players: Borovay: D'Andre Swift. Jordan: Colston Loveland.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Mahomes Depot

Breece Yourselves vs DAMNNN Daniel!!

Matt has won three straight and climbed back into the playoff hunt, while Suekoff is on track for the No. 1 pick. On paper, this is a mismatch. In practice, it smells like an upset. Matt will be without two of his highest scorers, with Bucky Irving on bye and Puka Nacua injured, and it is fair to wonder whether Brock Purdy and Ja'Marr Chase can carry the load alone. Suekoff is not exactly a high-scoring team, but if Ollie Gordon, KC Concepcion and Garrett Wilson keep playing well, he could sneak out a low-scoring upset.

Bye Week Players: Suekoff: Baker Mayfield, Emeka Egbuka. Matt: Bucky Irving.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Breece Yourselves

Unsolicited Dak Pics vs BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

The hottest team in the league meets one of the most erratic. Parker has won five straight behind Jordan Love, Christian McCaffrey, Christian Watson and Javonte Williams, and a second quarterback before the deadline could set up another deep playoff run. Charlie has been up and down all year and is one of just two GMs, along with Justin, to be both the highest- and lowest-scoring team in a given week. Which version shows up is anyone's guess. Amon-Ra St. Brown can win a matchup on his own. Kirk Cousins moving to Minnesota is a good idea on paper, but it may take him a few games to get up to speed. The streak continues.

Bye Week Players: Charlie: Jonah Coleman.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Unsolicited Dak Pics

The Idea of BO-lieve vs The Hurts Locker

Gembo arrives on a two-game win streak, a remarkable turn for a team that began 1-6, and a win here would put the playoffs within earshot. Rebecca is coming off a win of her own. Jahmyr Gibbs is the best player on the field by a wide margin and has the power to win this one almost single-handedly for Gembo, though it is fair to wonder how much help the rest of the roster will bring. Rebecca's squad is not as fancy, but Bryce Young, Jaylen Warren, DeMario Douglas and Malik Nabers make up a solid group, as long as they are all still on the team by Sunday.

Bye Week Players: Gembo: Bo Nix, Jaylen Waddle, Luther Burden. Rebecca: Rome Odunze.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: The Hurts Locker

Fat JaMarcus Russell vs Barking up the Wrong Tree

The most lopsided matchup of the week pits Justin, sitting at 7-2 atop the Shane Falco division, against Ross, who is 3-6 and the lowest-scoring team in the league. Justin still has a hole at RB2, but Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Tee Higgins and Chris Olave form one of the best receiver groups in the league, and he is sure to keep scouring the market for another back. Ross has had a tough, injury-filled year, and the path to an upset is narrow: Dak Prescott and Jared Goff both need monster games, A.J. Brown has to return to glory, and Chase Brown has to finally do what everyone expected of him. That is a lot of ifs, and it is hard to see it all happening this year.

Bye Week Players: Justin: Caleb Williams, Kyle Monangai. Ross: Saquon Barkley, Courtland Sutton.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Fat JaMarcus Russell
