Week 5 Recap

Borovay Is the Last Unbeaten Team Standing as Monday Night Decides Two Nail-Biters

By the League Newsroom · Week 5 Edition

Then there was one. Five weeks into the season, the league's field of unbeaten teams has been whittled to a single survivor, and his name is Borovay. He kept the perfect record intact without Patrick Mahomes, which only adds to the legend, while Justin's run finally came to a thunderous end at the hands of Jordan, in the biggest blowout of the week by more than 50 points. Nick and Justin sit at 4-1, Jordan and Robby at 3-2, and at the bottom, Rebecca, Gembo and Suekoff are all stuck at 1-4. The division races are taking shape too: Nick took over the Shane Falco Division, Jordan climbed to the top of the Bobbie Miles Division, and Borovay's grip on the Paul Crewe Division keeps tightening.

This week had two moods and nothing in between. On one side were two white-knuckle games that came down to Monday night. Nick was down by more than 20 points before Josh Allen turned the night upside down, and Ross escaped Charlie by a single point after the Rams defense went into the red at the worst possible moment. On the other side, everything else was a rout, with every remaining game decided by 20-plus points. Some weeks are a slow burn, and others are a bonfire. This was a bit of both. And while the dust settles, Justin refuses to sit still. Fresh off his first loss, he went shopping, landing running back Bhyshaul Tuten from Suekoff and wide receiver Chris Olave from Gembo. An unbeaten start can end in a hurry, but the way back starts with getting busy. Justin wasn't alone at the trade counter, either. Nick landed Drake Maye, fresh off a 40-point day, from Gembo, sending Fernando Mendoza, a 2027 first and a 2028 second the other way. With Trey McBride and Chris Olave gone too, Gembo has fully entered sell mode. Full breakdowns below.

Josh Allen Powers a Monday Night Comeback as Nick Tops Suekoff

Down more than 20 points entering Monday night, Nick needed a hero. He got Josh Allen. Allen threw for over 200 yards, ran for 110 more, and scored twice, and the comeback was on. Derrick Henry cleared 100 yards for the second time this year and added a touchdown. Justin Jefferson, back from injury, caught his first touchdown since Week 1. Sam LaPorta scored again, his third in the last four games, and Drake London chipped in 7 catches for 118 yards. Nick takes the lead in the Shane Falco Division. Final: Nick.

Suekoff couldn't have asked for more from his lineup, and it still wasn't enough. Jacoby Brissett tossed 3 touchdowns. Garrett Wilson posted his second 100-yard game and his third touchdown of the season. Aaron Jones ran for 113 yards and a score, Ollie Gordon scored for the third straight week, and Tyler Warren, a tight end, ran for a touchdown. The lone dud was Romeo Doubs, and that decision cost him: start Egbuka over Doubs and Suekoff wins. A great week with one bad lineup call.

— Colin Audible
Game of the Week Turns Into a Rout as Jordan Runs Past Justin

Billed as the game of the week, it turned into a one-sided affair. Jordan answered every question put to him, starting at quarterback, where Jayden Daniels went for more than 300 yards of total offense and added 2 rushing touchdowns. Mike Evans delivered his best game of the year, 7 catches for 113 yards and a score. Trey McBride, playing his first game for his new GM, made a strong first impression with 9 catches and a touchdown. Even with Bijan Robinson bottled up for most of the afternoon, he still managed 118 total yards. That was more than enough to hand Jordan the win, and the Bobbie Miles Division lead.

Justin, meanwhile, had a rough day and the lowest score in the league this week, and the injuries did the damage. Matthew Stafford threw 3 more touchdowns in his best game of the year, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba posted his fourth 100-yard day and scored for the seventh time. Outside of those two, there wasn't much to work with. With TreVeyon Henderson hurt after only 22 yards, and Brian Robinson unable to match the three scores he had a week earlier, the backfield came up empty. Justin falls for the first time this season, and he's in need of running back help. (And he has already gone out and done just that, trading for Bhyshaul Tuten. More on that in the Week 6 preview.)

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Borovay Survives a Mahomes Bye and Stays Perfect at 5-0

Borovay is the last unbeaten team standing, and he got there without his quarterback. Patrick Mahomes was on a bye, and QB2 Tyler Shough finished in negative points, which is a trick in itself. It didn't matter. Aaron Rodgers stepped in and threw 3 touchdowns. Parker Washington exploded for 124 yards and 2 scores. James Cook fumbled twice and still rushed for 111 yards and a pair of touchdowns on Monday night, which is the kind of thing only a team this deep can absorb. And Brock Bowers keeps doing what he does: 3 games played, 3 touchdowns caught, and a case for the best tight end in the league.

For Robby, the three-game win streak ends, and the data points to a familiar problem: his young running backs. Jeremiyah Love ran for just 22 yards and hasn't scored since Week 3. Ashton Jeanty managed 27 yards and hasn't found the end zone since Week 1. Harold Fannin Jr. caught one pass. The quarterbacks did their part, with Joe Burrow throwing 4 touchdowns and Trevor Lawrence adding 2, and it still wasn't close to enough. For the first time this season, Robby didn't crack 100 points.

— Ivana Blitz
Matt Snaps His Skid in the Battle of the 1-3s

Not a perfect day, but the stars showed up. Puka Nacua set a career high with 17 catches, 138 yards and a score. Bucky Irving ran for 175 yards and 2 touchdowns, more fantasy points than his last three weeks combined. Ja'Marr Chase caught his fourth touchdown of the year, and Brock Purdy tossed a pair. The Jets defense chipped in 8 sacks and 2 fumble recoveries while allowing just 12 points. Matt snaps the skid and saves his season. For now. Final: Matt.

Rebecca got big games from Jaylen Warren (188 total yards, 2 touchdowns), Malik Nabers (11 catches, 121 yards and a score, clearly clicking with Winston) and Matthew Golden, who set a career high with 123 receiving yards. It wasn't enough. Dalton Kincaid has faded after his incredible start, with just 4 catches over the last 3 weeks. Jalen Hurts hasn't topped 15 points in 3 straight, and Justin Herbert is just bad. Rebecca falls to 1-4 and is on the brink.

— Colin Audible
A Negative Night for the Rams D Saves Ross and Sinks Charlie

It looked over before Monday night even began. Ross had nobody left to play, and Charlie, with Kyren Williams and the Rams defense still to go, trailed by just 15. Yet Ross is still standing, and the quarterbacks did it again. Jared Goff had his best game of the year, throwing three scores and running in another. Dak Prescott had his worst game and still posted 316 yards and a touchdown. The breakout came from Braelon Allen, who ran for 116 yards and a score, and Emanuel Wilson found the end zone for the second straight game. Saquon Barkley is inching toward a return this weekend. Maybe, just maybe, there's hope for Ross.

For Charlie, it was a gut punch. Kyren did his job, 76 yards and a score, and handed him the lead. Then the Rams defense went negative, and the game slipped away by a single point. It was the lowest-scoring and closest matchup of the week. The box score isn't pretty, but it had bright spots: Isaiah Likely had his best game since Week 1 with 2 touchdowns, and MarShawn Lloyd finally found the end zone, twice. Cam Ward regressed with 170 yards and an interception, and Amon-Ra St. Brown had his third straight ordinary game.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Parker Lights Up the Scoreboard and Joins the Hunt

The highest-scoring game of the week, and Parker owned the top score of it. Everything clicked up and down the lineup. Christian Watson hauled in 143 yards and 2 scores, his sixth touchdown of the year. George Pickens had his best game, 9 catches for 130 yards and a touchdown. Jordan Love had his best game, 367 yards and 2 touchdowns, and Kyler Murray had his own best game, with 229 passing yards, 51 rushing yards and a score. Christian McCaffrey scored for the fourth straight week, and when it takes this long to get to CMC, that's a very good sign. Last year's runner-up is back in the hunt.

Gembo dropped his fourth straight, and the numbers tell an odd story. He was a top-five scorer this week and still lost. Maybe another ember for the league median scoring debate. The total looks great, but it was propped up by just a few players: Drake Maye, who remembered how to play football with 374 yards and 4 touchdowns for a 40-point day, and Jahmyr Gibbs, who piled up over 200 total yards and 2 scores. Spencer Shrader and the Broncos defense did the rest. No other starter topped 8 points. With Trey McBride, Chris Olave and now Drake Maye all out the door, the sell-off is officially on for Gembo.

— Ivana Blitz

Get Griddy With It vs Barking up the Wrong Tree

Best offense in the league against the worst. Nick averages 147.5 points a game. Ross averages 97.1. On paper, it's a mismatch. Nick just added Drake Maye as his QB2, and he's been heating up. He now pairs him with Josh Allen. Derrick Henry and Chuba Hubbard give him a backfield that's rolling, and Tetairoa McMillan will try to follow up his career day. Ross's week starts in the training room. Is Saquon Barkley playing? What about Ladd McConkey? Emmanuel Wilson has been a bright spot and is chasing a touchdown in a third straight game. But his quarterbacks haven't been game changers like Allen or Maye. Neither Dak Prescott nor Michael Penix has thrown multiple touchdowns in a game since Dak did it in Week 2. Ross doesn't have the firepower to keep pace. Nick rolls.

Bye Week Players: Nick: Justin Jefferson, Sam LaPorta, Will Reichard. Ross: Jared Goff, Chase Brown.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Get Griddy With It

Jayden's Mambo No. 4s vs The Hurts Locker

For Rebecca, this is desperation week. At 1-4, her season hangs by a thread, but the thread is stronger than it looks: she sits just one game behind the final playoff spot, and a win would pull her right back into the conversation. The firepower is there. Jonathan Taylor, Jaylen Warren, Malik Nabers and Matthew Golden are all playing well. What she needs is more from under center. Jalen Hurts hasn't topped 17 points since Week 1, and the elite quarterback she bargained for has yet to arrive. A healthy Breece Hall would be a welcome boost, too. Across the table, Jordan arrives riding high after toppling the previously unbeaten Justin. His backfield features two top-five running backs in Bijan Robinson and Kenneth Walker, Trey McBride made a splash in his first game with the team, and Lamar Jackson is on track to play. In a shootout, Jordan has the bigger guns.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

DAMNNN Daniel!! vs Unsolicited Dak Pics

Two 2-3 teams, two wins that kept the season alive, and exactly one way out with a .500 record. Neither side arrives at full strength. Matt is without Ja'Marr Chase, and Parker is without Kyler Murray and a Vikings D/ST that has averaged 15 points a game. On the field, Parker is coming off his best week of the year and leans on the Jordan Love-Christian Watson connection, which has produced 6 touchdowns, plus a top-10 backfield of Javonte Williams and Christian McCaffrey. The unlock would be DJ Moore, who has yet to deliver. Matt got career days from Bucky Irving and Puka Nacua last week, and with Chase on bye he'll need both to do it again. A flash from Woody Marks or a returning Jalen Coker would help, too. Matt can't just hope lightning strikes twice. Parker has the steadier path to .500.

Bye Week Players: Matt: Ja'Marr Chase. Parker: Kyler Murray, Vikings D/ST.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Unsolicited Dak Pics

BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY vs Mahomes Depot

The league's last unbeaten team is two wins from matching its best start ever, 7-0. Brock Bowers has scored in every game he's played, with 100 receiving yards in two of three. James Cook has a fumbling problem, but he's scored in five straight. CeeDee Lamb was always going to be elite. Parker Washington's breakout wasn't in the plan, but it's a big reason for the 5-0 start. Charlie was one negative defensive performance away from 3-2. Instead, he's coming off a loss with some bright spots. Isaiah Likely scored twice. MarShawn Lloyd finally found the end zone. Kyren Williams remains steady. But Cam Ward and Tony Pollard haven't produced, and that caps this roster. Add the bye for Amon-Ra St. Brown, his biggest breakout, and the margin is thin. Borovay keeps rolling.

Bye Week Players: Charlie: Amon-Ra St. Brown. Borovay: Jameson Williams.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Mahomes Depot

Baker's Bunch vs Breece Yourselves

Two managers, two losses, and one uncomfortable Wednesday. Robby is coming off his worst showing of the season, and Suekoff was a single Monday night performance away from a win. Suekoff has already tipped his hand by trading away Bhyshaul Tuten, a sign he may be eyeing next season, and this week could tip the scale. To keep the season alive, he'll lean on Rhamondre Stevenson, who has scored in two straight, Jacoby Brissett, fresh off a season-best three-touchdown day, and Garrett Wilson and Tyler Warren, both coming off big weeks. Robby gets help in DeVonta Smith, who is on track to return and join Davante Adams and Zay Flowers. He'll need it, because rookie backs Ashton Jeanty and Jeremiyah Love have been a roller coaster.

Bye Week Players: Robby: Joe Burrow. Suekoff: Aaron Jones, Ollie Gordon.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Baker's Bunch

Fat JaMarcus Russell vs The Idea of BO-lieve

Same league, opposite ends of the spectrum. Justin's perfect season is gone, but he didn't sit around mourning it. He worked the phones, landing Bhyshaul Tuten in one deal and Chris Olave in another. Gembo was on the other end of that second one. After moving Olave, Trey McBride and Drake Maye, Gembo's eyes are clearly on next year, when he holds five picks in the first two rounds. On paper, this is a blowout. Gembo's one remaining elite player, Jahmyr Gibbs, is on bye, and so is Jordan Addison. The math on a Gembo upset requires everything to break right for him and wrong for Justin. We wouldn't bet on it.

Bye Week Players: Justin: Tee Higgins. Gembo: Jahmyr Gibbs, Jordan Addison.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Fat JaMarcus Russell
