Week 10 Recap

Favorites Rule Week 10 as Parker Posts the Top Score

By the League Newsroom · Week 10 Edition

Parker had the top score of the week and rolled to his sixth straight win, pulling within one game of Justin in the Shane Falco Division. Justin held him off with a second straight win of his own. Borovay beat Jordan to stay on top of the league and is now just one win, or one Gembo loss, from clinching the Paul Crewe Division. Robby took down Nick to hold the No. 3 seed, one game ahead of Matt in the Bobbie Miles Division.

Matt won his fourth straight in the blowout of the week over Suekoff, and he is back within a game of the division lead. Jordan fell to .500 and clings to the final playoff spot, while Nick has dropped four straight and slipped out of the field. Gembo is making a late push with three straight wins, and Rebecca is just about eliminated. With four weeks left and the trade deadline extended to Nov. 25, the playoff race is far from settled.

Robby's Quarterbacks Keep the Wins Coming

Robby kept it rolling, winning four of his last five, and he did it with quarterback play once again. Joe Burrow is on one right now, throwing for more than 400 yards and 4 more touchdowns. Over the last 4 weeks, he has 18 total touchdowns. Trevor Lawrence was not overshadowed, tossing 352 yards and 3 touchdowns for his fifth game with three or more. Deebo Samuel returned from injury and found the end zone twice. Robby still needs more from his running backs. Final: Robby.

Nick fell back to .500 with a fourth straight loss. The mood took a hit early when Drake Maye went down with an apparent ankle injury and is expected to miss some time. The team wasted a big day from Chuba Hubbard, who had 172 total yards and 2 touchdowns. Josh Allen scored 3 more touchdowns, but it was not enough. The bright spot was Justin Jefferson, who found his groove again with Kirk Cousins.

— Colin Audible
Borovay Stays on Top of the League

Borovay, the highest-scoring team in the league, kept right on rolling, and the man leading the charge was Patrick Mahomes. He threw for 348 yards and a season-high 5 touchdowns. His running backs were just as dynamic. James Cook rushed for a season-high 157 yards and a score, extending his run of scoring games, while Cam Skattebo climbed out of the doghouse with more than 120 yards and 2 touchdowns. Parker Washington added a season-high 152 yards and a touchdown. It was another reminder of why Borovay sits on top of the league.

Jordan lost for the third time in his last four games and dropped to .500, though this was a respectable showing. Injuries to Lamar Jackson, Jayden Daniels and Kenneth Walker have played a part in the ups and downs of his season. Daniels threw for a season-high 3 touchdowns, but also tossed 2 interceptions, and C.J. Stroud threw 3 touchdowns of his own. Blake Corum hit 100 yards for the first time this year and has now scored in 6 straight, filling in nicely for Walker. It was a solid week for Jordan, but he ran up against the best team in the league.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
All-or-Nothing Matt Cruises to a Fourth Straight Win

The blowout of the week went to Matt, who won his fourth straight and climbed back within a game of the division lead. The formula was simple: a few massive performances and not much else. Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 passes for an insane 231 yards, Brock Purdy threw for 389 yards and 3 touchdowns, and Deshaun Watson added 312 yards and a touchdown. Most of the rest of the lineup failed to top 10 points. With Puka Nacua on injured reserve, Matt needs some help at receiver if he wants to keep this run going.

A seventh straight loss for Suekoff, who has now taken the crown as the lowest-scoring team in the league. Garrett Wilson is playing well, topping 100 receiving yards for the third time, and Ollie Gordon is making the most of his playing time with 115 total yards. The rest of the roster has been stripped down. The No. 1 pick is within grasp, and the long season continues.

— Ivana Blitz
Parker Tops the Scoreboard and Closes In on Justin

Parker was the top scorer of the week, taking down the No. 2 scorer in Charlie. He has now won 6 in a row and sits just one game back of Justin for first in the division. Two monster performances led the way. Christian McCaffrey had 223 total yards and 3 touchdowns, and George Pickens hauled in a season-high 11 catches for 145 yards and 3 scores. The trade for Jared Goff made an immediate impact, as he tossed 303 yards and 3 touchdowns. Final: Parker.

Charlie did everything he could. He scored the second-most points of the week and ran into the only team that scored more. Isaiah Likely had a season-high 127 yards and a touchdown, and Amon-Ra St. Brown had his best game since Week 2 with 98 yards and 2 scores. Tony Pollard keeps producing, with 99 total yards and a touchdown. The Rams D/ST has become a bit of a weapon, with a third straight game of 14 or more points. It was not quite enough.

— Colin Audible
Gembo Plays With House Money as Gibbs Does It All

After all but mailing in the season, Gembo has now won three straight and is suddenly back in the hunt. The engine of this turnaround is Jahmyr Gibbs, who is carrying it almost by himself. Gibbs rushed for 153 yards and 2 touchdowns and added 99 receiving yards on top. The rest of the team was fairly ordinary. Drew Lock tossed 2 touchdowns, and Fernando Mendoza ran for one in his debut. But Gibbs was more than enough, and Gembo is playing with house money at this point.

Rebecca fell to 2-8 and is just about eliminated from the playoff race. Recent acquisition Quinshon Judkins had a solid first game for his new team, finding the end zone, and Breece Hall topped 100 total yards. It was a fine effort from a couple of backs, but it all feels a little too little, too late.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Justin Holds the Division Lead Behind Big Days From His Skill Players

Justin won his second straight and held on to first place in the division. The engine, as usual, was Jaxon Smith-Njigba, the No. 1 receiver in the league, who hauled in 8 catches for a season-high 171 yards and a score. Bhayshul Tuten topped 100 yards and scored for a fourth straight game. Emmett Johnson had a monster day too, piling up 171 yards and 2 touchdowns. Justin remains one game ahead of Parker, and the backfield looks a lot less like a problem than it did a few weeks ago.

Ross lost his third straight, but this was his second-highest scoring output of the season, and it was enough to lift him out of the cellar as the lowest-scoring team in the league. Dak Prescott did most of the work, throwing for 343 yards and 5 touchdowns. Chase Brown had his best game of the year with 83 total yards and 2 touchdowns, his first scores since Week 1. It was the kind of day the roster has been missing all season, and it came a few weeks too late.

— Ivana Blitz

Week 11 Matchups
This is the final week of non-division play. After this, the season wraps up with three straight games against division foes, and with some of the divisions this tight, every one of them will count.
Writer's Record: 28-32 on the season (3-3 in Week 10).

Get Griddy With It vs BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

Both teams are fighting for their playoff lives. Nick has lost four straight and is on the outside looking in at 5-5, while Charlie is one game out of the final spot after a monster performance that still resulted in a loss. Nick has a quarterback problem. With Drake Maye hurt, he turns to Geno Smith, if healthy, at QB2, and Josh Allen and Chuba Hubbard can only do so much. The bright spot is Justin Jefferson, who should be even better in his second week with Kirk Cousins. Charlie has been scoring a lot of points lately. Jonah Coleman is emerging in the Denver backfield and adding depth alongside a productive Tony Pollard, and Cam Ward is playing better. This one goes to the team scoring more right now.

Bye Week Players: Nick: Drake London, Drake Maye. Charlie: Kyren Williams, Rams D/ST.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: BRICKED4BRANDONAUBREY

Breece Yourselves vs Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

This is a game Jordan must win, and should. He has lost three of his last four and has slipped to the No. 6 seed, despite ranking second in the league in total points. It has been a strange year for a team with this much firepower, but help is on the way. Kenneth Walker should be back, and C.J. Stroud has filled in nicely for Lamar Jackson. On paper, this is about as lopsided as it gets, though Jordan has not exactly played to the paper this season. Suekoff, meanwhile, has little left to play for. He has lost seven straight, is out of playoff contention, and has become the lowest-scoring team in the league. He is the current leader for the No. 1 pick, and the best he can do is play spoiler, which is a tall order without the firepower to pull it off.

Bye Week Players: Jordan: Bijan Robinson, Blake Corum, Denzel Boston. Suekoff: KC Concepcion, Rhamondre Stevenson.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Jayden's Mambo No. 4s

The Idea of BO-lieve vs DAMNNN Daniel!!

This is a much different matchup than it would have been a few weeks ago. Matt has won four straight and climbed within a game of the Bobbie Miles Division lead, while Gembo has won three in a row and, somehow, after selling, is within spitting distance of a playoff spot. Can Gembo keep the wild run alive? That rests on Jahmyr Gibbs, a one-man force, but the question is whether the rest of the roster can chip in, with players like Seth McGowan and Jaylen Waddle. Matt has been top-heavy during this run, leaning hard on Bucky Irving, Ja'Marr Chase and Brock Purdy, and with Puka Nacua out for the foreseeable future, he needs that to continue. David Montgomery is back this week and could provide a much-needed jolt of depth.

Bye Week Players: Gembo: Jadarian Price, Drew Lock. Matt: Deshaun Watson.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: DAMNNN Daniel!!

Fat JaMarcus Russell vs Baker's Bunch

Our marquee matchup of the week features two division leaders who are also fighting for a first-round bye, with teams nipping at the heels of both. Robby has gotten insane quarterback play from Joe Burrow and Trevor Lawrence, and he has needed it with the up-and-down play of his running backs, Jonathan Taylor and Ashton Jeanty. The good news is that DeVonta Smith might return this week. Justin will need all the help he can get, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Matthew Stafford on bye and Joe Milton starting at QB. With Kenneth Walker back, Emmett Johnson will not have as big a role, but Tee Higgins is due for another monster game the way Burrow is playing. The edge at quarterback goes to Robby.

Bye Week Players: Justin: Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Matthew Stafford. Robby: Harold Fannin Jr., Matthew Golden.

— Colin Audible
Writer's Pick: Baker's Bunch

Barking up the Wrong Tree vs Unsolicited Dak Pics

Opposite ends of the spectrum meet here. Parker has won six straight and is within a game of both the division lead and a first-round bye, and it starts and ends with Christian McCaffrey, who has stayed healthy for two years running. He has more help this year in George Pickens and Javonte Williams, and Jared Goff has solidified the quarterback room. Ross has lost three straight, needs to win out just to keep slim hopes alive, and will be hit hard by byes. Chase Brown is coming off his best game, but injuries and poor play have plagued Ross from the start.

Bye Week Players: Parker: Christian Watson, Jordan Love, Jason Myers. Ross: Zach Charbonnet, A.J. Brown, Tucker Kraft, Michael Penix, Tutu Atwell.

— Frank Lee Hurtz
Writer's Pick: Unsolicited Dak Pics

Mahomes Depot vs The Hurts Locker

Borovay is healthy and an absolute tank of a team. He leads the league in scoring and has no weaknesses anywhere on the roster. Patrick Mahomes is playing his best fantasy football in years, and the recent resurgence of Cam Skattebo gives Borovay a dominant three-headed monster with Skattebo, James Cook and D'Andre Swift, not to mention Brock Bowers. The only hiccup has been the declining play of Tyler Shough. Rebecca is not technically out of it, but a loss this week all but seals her fate. She has already traded away most of her high-end talent, and while Malik Nabers is elite, the rest of the roster has not been good enough to win her enough games.

Bye Week Players: Rebecca: Quinshon Judkins, Kyle Pitts Sr., DeMario Douglas.

— Ivana Blitz
Writer's Pick: Mahomes Depot
